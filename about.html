Our story

Lahore is a city best enjoyed at your own pace. We wanted visitors to have a comfortable place to return to after a day out, with the space and little comforts of home. So we put together a collection of welcoming stays in Gulberg, DHA and Johar Town, and a friendly local team to help make each visit simple.

Whether you are here for work, family or a change of scene, we are glad to host you.
